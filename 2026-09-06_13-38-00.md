# Update: Website Redesign (Modern Glassmorphism)
**Date**: 2026-09-06

## Changes Made
1. **Design System Update**: Stripped out the generic template look. Upgraded the entire `style.css` to a modern, minimalist aesthetic heavily favored by top AI companies.
2. **Color Palette**: 
   - Background forced to pure black (`#000000`).
   - Accent color switched to vibrant blue (`#1971ff`).
3. **Glassmorphism**: Added heavy `backdrop-filter` blurring on the navbar, feature cards, and footer, giving a sleek frosted-glass effect over the background.
4. **Ambient Glow**: Injected `.bg-elements` (animated glowing orbs) into `index.html` and `about.html` that slowly float behind the frosted glass cards to create depth.
5. **Typography & UX**: Applied smooth scrolling, larger display typography with tight letter spacing (`letter-spacing: -2px`), and modernized buttons to pill-shapes with soft drop shadows.
