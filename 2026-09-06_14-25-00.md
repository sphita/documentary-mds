# Update: Comprehensive SEO & Discoverability Pass
**Date**: 2026-09-06

## Changes Made
1. **Search Engine Crawlability**:
   - Generated and deployed a `robots.txt` file at the root to instruct crawlers and explicitly link the sitemap.
   - Built a comprehensive `sitemap.xml` mapping the exact URL structure, priority, and update frequencies of all core pages and blog routes.
2. **Schema.org Structured Data**:
   - Injected `application/ld+json` (JSON-LD) Organization markup into the `<head>` of all HTML files. This explicitly tells Google about the Sphita brand, linking the official URL, social profiles (GitHub, HF), and the official Pirate HF logo as the brand logo.
3. **Open Graph & Twitter Cards**:
   - Upgraded all pages with rigorous social meta tags (`og:site_name`, `og:image`, `twitter:card`, `twitter:image`) to ensure that links shared on Discord, Twitter, or LinkedIn render massive, beautiful preview cards featuring the logo.
